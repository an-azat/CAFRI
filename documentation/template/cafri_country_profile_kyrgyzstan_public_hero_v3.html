About the country

Geography, people and economic structure

The Kyrgyz Republic is a landlocked country in Central Asia, bordered by Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and China. Its territory is dominated by the Tian Shan mountain system, while the Chüy and Fergana valleys host important population centres and economic activity.

The economy combines services, trade, agriculture, mining and hydropower. Its regional position, remittance flows, cross-border commerce and exposure to external demand are important factors shaping economic developments.

COUNTRY ANALYSIS

Macroeconomic assessment

The Kyrgyz Republic continues to record strong economic growth, while elevated inflation and expanding domestic demand remain important policy considerations.

01
Economic momentum

Real GDP grew by 10.8% year-on-year in January–September 2026. The pace of expansion remains strong, supported by domestic activity and investment.

02
Price stability

Annual inflation eased to 10.8% in October from 12.2% in September, but remains above the NBKR's medium-term target range.

03
Fiscal position

The first-half budget surplus provides a positive near-term fiscal signal. The rising public-debt ratio and expenditure trajectory remain important to monitor.

Based on National Statistical Committee, NBKR and World Bank publications. This is CAFRI editorial analysis, not an official country rating.
